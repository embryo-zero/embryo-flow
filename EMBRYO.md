# Embryo Flow

Embryo Flow is Embryo-Zero's fork of [OpenFOAM](https://github.com/OpenFOAM/OpenFOAM-dev) (20261113, commit fed424ac). It does fluid flow and heat simulation.

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (COPYING) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
